# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want {{PROJECT_NAME}} to be a welcoming space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in speech and actions.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official communication channels — and also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at **TODO: contact email or link**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and will be kept confidential to the extent possible.

Maintainers who violate this Code of Conduct in good faith reports may face the same consequences as any other contributor.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

1. **Correction** — private, written warning for a minor first incident.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for sustained or severe violations.
